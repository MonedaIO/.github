# Moneda

**AI Does the Research. You Make the Call. That's Co-Investing.**

[Moneda](https://moneda.com) is an investing app made in Europe. Ask Moneda AI, the agent, about a stock or your own portfolio. It reads company filings, earnings calls and market data and shows you where every number comes from, with the case for and against. When you have decided, you invest in US-listed stocks, ETFs or crypto in the same app, approving every trade with your passkey.

The agent runs on [Numa 1](https://moneda.com/models/numa-1), our financial AI model. Its benchmark results are public.

## For developers

Your Moneda account is also available to the tools you already use, with permissions you set:

- **[Moneda MCP](https://docs.moneda.com/mcp-overview)**: connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client to your account.
- **[REST API](https://docs.moneda.com/api/overview)**: read your portfolio and data, and request actions you approve in the app.
- **[CLI](https://docs.moneda.com/cli/overview)**: the same from your terminal.

Start with the [quickstart](https://docs.moneda.com/getting-started/quickstart), or read the full docs at [docs.moneda.com](https://docs.moneda.com).

## Open source

Moneda accounts are self-custodial smart accounts. We publish the recovery work behind them:

- [social-recovery](https://github.com/MonedaIO/social-recovery): smart account modules, audits, tests and formal verification
- [email-recovery](https://github.com/MonedaIO/email-recovery): account recovery for any smart wallet with ZK Email (ERC-7579 and ERC-4337)
- [relayer-utils](https://github.com/MonedaIO/relayer-utils), [relayer-imap](https://github.com/MonedaIO/relayer-imap), [relayer-smtp](https://github.com/MonedaIO/relayer-smtp): Rust building blocks for ZK Email relayers
